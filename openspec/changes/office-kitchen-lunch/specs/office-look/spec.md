## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A full kitchen
The far wall SHALL hold a kitchen with a fridge, upper and lower cabinets, a sink and a hob. A kitchen island SHALL stand in front of it with bar stools on its near side.

#### Scenario: Looking down the hall
- **WHEN** you stand at the walkway and look at the far end
- **THEN** you see a kitchen along the far wall with an island in front of it

### Requirement: A lunch island
The open space at the far end SHALL hold two long tables side by side with chairs down both outer sides. The walkway SHALL stay clear whatever the number of sessions is.

#### Scenario: Walking around
- **WHEN** you walk to the far end
- **THEN** you can pass the lunch island on either side and the tables and chairs block you like any other furniture
