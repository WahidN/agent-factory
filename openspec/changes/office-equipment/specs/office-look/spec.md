## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Kitchen appliances
The kitchen SHALL hold an espresso machine, a kettle and a toaster on the counter, a microwave built into the tall cabinet, and a dishwasher front between the lower cabinets next to the sink. They SHALL stay inside the kitchen's existing footprint, so the floor in front of it stays as free as it was.

#### Scenario: Looking at the counter
- **WHEN** you stand at the island and look at the kitchen
- **THEN** you see the espresso machine, kettle and toaster on the counter, the microwave in the tall cabinet and a steel dishwasher front under the counter

### Requirement: A keyboard and a mouse at every desk
Every desk SHALL have a keyboard and a mouse in front of its monitor, next to the laptop. A desk that appears or disappears SHALL take its keyboard and mouse with it.

#### Scenario: Four sessions
- **WHEN** the user has four sessions
- **THEN** each of the four desks has one keyboard and one mouse

### Requirement: Office equipment in the hall
Every meeting room, the door-end room included, SHALL have a screen on a stand at the far end of its table, facing it. The far end SHALL have a floor-standing printer beside the walkway and a whiteboard on wheels next to the round table. The screens, the printer and the whiteboard SHALL block you like other furniture, and the walkway SHALL stay clear whatever the number of sessions is.

#### Scenario: Meeting rooms
- **WHEN** the hall has three meeting rooms along the desk zone
- **THEN** each of them and the door-end room has one screen facing its table

#### Scenario: Walking to the far end
- **WHEN** you walk down the walkway to the far end
- **THEN** you pass the printer without bumping into it, and you cannot walk through the printer, the whiteboard or a screen

### Requirement: Meeting rooms you can walk into
Every meeting room, the door-end room included, SHALL have a doorway in its glass wall at the end of the room nearest the door, wide enough to walk through, with a glass door standing open beside it. The table SHALL run down the length of the room with its chairs along both long sides.

#### Scenario: Walking in
- **WHEN** you walk up to the doorway of a meeting room and keep walking
- **THEN** you end up inside the room, and the rest of the glass wall still blocks you

#### Scenario: Table along the room
- **WHEN** you look into a meeting room
- **THEN** the table runs from the door end toward the screen, with two chairs on each long side facing it
